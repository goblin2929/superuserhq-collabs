# AI Training Materials

Reusable AI / agentic-workflow training assets that can be re-skinned and redelivered across workshops (tiket, SMU, TMS, and future clients). Build once, adapt per audience — don't rebuild from scratch.

## How to reuse

Each deck is audience-agnostic in the body; only the title slide, footer crumb, and closing need per-audience edits. When repurposing:
- Update the title slide eyebrow/footer (client name, city, date).
- Swap any client-specific demo examples for ones relevant to the new audience.
- Keep the closing LinkedIn QR slide (Tina + Yaohong) unless told otherwise.
